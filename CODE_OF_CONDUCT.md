# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep discussions on topic, and never post other people's private data, credentials or internal network details.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer at kamyar.rp@gmail.com. The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who do not follow these rules.
